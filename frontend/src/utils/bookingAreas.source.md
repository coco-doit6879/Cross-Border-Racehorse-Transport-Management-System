Ward data for the three Vietnamese service stops is bundled locally so booking does not depend on a third party request at runtime.

Source: https://provinces.open-api.vn/api/v2/ (post-July 2025 administrative divisions), retrieved 2026-10-25.

Province endpoints: `p/79?depth=2` (Ho Chi Minh City), `p/1?depth=2` (Hanoi), `p/48?depth=2` (Da Nang).

International stops may supply `areas` in the transport catalog; otherwise their area field allows manual entry.
